Sounza Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 4, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Sounza ("Sounza", "we", "us", or "our") handles information when you use the Sounza mobile application.

By using Sounza, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

Sounza may process certain information required to provide, maintain, secure, and improve the application.

Depending on how you use the application and the services available on your device, this may include:

  • Device and application information.
  • IP address and approximate network information.
  • Advertising identifiers, such as the Android Advertising ID, where permitted.
  • Basic technical information such as operating system version, app version, and device type.
  • Information related to advertising interactions and application usage.
  • Information that you voluntarily provide when contacting us.

2. Advertising

Sounza may display advertisements through third-party advertising services in order to support the application and its continued development.

Advertising providers may process certain information, such as device information, advertising identifiers, IP address, and advertising-related events, in accordance with their own privacy policies and applicable laws.

Where required by applicable law, appropriate consent mechanisms may be provided before personalized advertising is enabled.

3. Advertising Identifier

Sounza and its advertising providers may use the Android Advertising ID for advertising and measurement purposes where permitted by applicable laws and device settings.

You may be able to reset or limit the use of your advertising identifier through your Android device settings.

4. IP Address and Technical Information

IP addresses and technical information may be processed by Sounza and relevant service providers for purposes such as delivering advertisements, preventing fraud and abuse, maintaining security, troubleshooting, and measuring application performance.

5. How We Use Information

Information may be used for the following purposes:

  • To provide and operate Sounza.
  • To provide and measure advertising services.
  • To maintain application security and prevent abuse.
  • To diagnose technical problems and improve the application.
  • To respond to support requests.
  • To comply with applicable legal obligations.

6. Third-Party Services

Sounza may use third-party services to provide advertising, music/content functionality, technical services, security, or other application features.

These third-party providers may process information according to their own privacy policies and applicable laws.

Sounza may use third-party advertising services, including Google Mobile Ads, for displaying advertisements within the application.

7. Consent and Privacy Choices

Where required by applicable privacy laws, Sounza may request your consent before certain types of personal information are processed for advertising or other purposes.

Your available privacy choices may depend on your location, device, and applicable regulations.

If a consent management interface is presented in the application, you can use it to review or manage the choices available to you.

8. Data Retention

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy, to provide the application and its services, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce applicable agreements.

Third-party service providers may have their own data retention policies.

9. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information processed through Sounza. However, no method of transmission or electronic storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

10. Children's Privacy

Sounza is not intentionally designed to collect personal information from children.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable privacy laws.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us so that we can review the situation and take appropriate action where required.

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights regarding your personal information, including rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to certain processing.

If you are located in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or another jurisdiction where GDPR-like protections apply, certain processing activities may require your consent or rely on another lawful basis.

13. California and Other U.S. Privacy Rights

Depending on your location and applicable U.S. privacy laws, you may have additional rights regarding the collection, use, sharing, or disclosure of personal information.
